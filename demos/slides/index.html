av2codec · av2 v1.0.0 · multiview

Four views.
One bitstream.
A canvas.

AV2 carries multiview as independent extended layers; a layer configuration record and an atlas say what each layer is and where it lands. This demo encodes four layers — three sports feeds and your camera — into one multistream and composes them into a single canvas.

  • One AV2 multistream, four extended layers
  • Every canvas frame reconstructed from the bitstream
  • Runs entirely in the browser — no server, no native code

try it

Your camera,
layer 3

Scan the code, open the page, press Enable camera. The tab encodes your webcam with the three feeds, decodes the multistream and paints the 640×384 canvas at 15 fps.

Every session can be downloaded as an .obu file — a real AV2 multistream with LCR and atlas.
